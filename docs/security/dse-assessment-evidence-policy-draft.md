# DSE Assessment Evidence Collection and Retention Policy
Version 0.1 — proposed for DSE/RUPP approval. Not an approved institutional policy.

## Purpose and authority
Maintain traceable student assessment evidence for AUN-QA Programme Level V4.0 Criterion 4 and internal continuous improvement. This is a proposed programme operational policy, subordinate to applicable RUPP academic, records, privacy and security requirements. Do not enable production collection until the responsible institutional authority confirms consistency with those requirements and records approval. The proposed sample count is not an AUN-QA mandatory rule.
Reference: AUN-QA Guide to Assessment at Programme Level V4.0, Criterion 4, https://www.aunsec.org/application/files/2816/7290/3752/Guide_to_AUN-QA_Assessment_at_Programme_Level_Version_4.0_4.pdf

## Collection and verification
- For each applicable major assessment per offering and academic term, select up to three authentic marked samples representing relative low, middle and high performance where available. Keep all official grade records according to institutional rules; evidence sampling does not replace grading records.
- Small cohorts: use available distinct eligible sources, record reasons for fewer samples; distinguish ties, absent submissions, assessed zero and missing grades. No fabricated work or claims of full completeness based on absent results.
- Group projects: one shared artifact per selected group and separately selected individual contributions or defence evidence where assessed. Preserve exact approved CourseSpec, assessment, locked membership, marks, rubric/marking scheme and feedback provenance. Avoid copying shared work per student.
- Attendance-only or other non-applicable items: coordinator records applicability and reason in an approved register. Dashboard currently cannot automate these exceptions; never describe its sample gap as an accreditation score.
- Lecturer confirms actual marked work, restricted access, and source linkage; independent coordinator verifies or requests changes. Contributors cannot self-review. Corrections invalidate affected verification; preserve historical revisions.
- Attach only necessary material. Redact extraneous personal data and use restricted links or private scanned storage. No public shares, credentials or duplicate bulk collection. Students should be informed of purpose, access and retention under RUPP requirements.

## Access and security
- Assigned lecturers and co-lecturers collect and access their offerings; scoped coordinator/authorized admin reviews. No public, student or unrelated lecturer access to this internal repository.
- Private bucket with explicit client denial, short-lived download, backend authorization, current malware scanner, approved file types and limits; audit and monitor use without logging sensitive file contents or secrets. Signed links are short-lived bearer capabilities, not shareable permanent URLs.
- External assessment reviewers receive only approved, minimized/de-identified extracts through an authorized controlled channel. No automatic external access; verify provider permission and file stability. Do not publish student grades or marked work.
- Handle suspected access breach by restricting access, preserving relevant audit records, notifying the institutional data/security owner and following approved incident procedures.

## Retention proposal — decision required
- Official assessment and grade records follow existing institutional retention requirements, not this proposal.
- Proposed starting rule for representative evidence: retain through the relevant accreditation cycle and any appeal/review or legal hold, then review annually for deletion or continued documented need. Exact maximum retention, start event and exceptions must be specified by authorized RUPP records owner before production activation. No indefinite-by-default retention and no premature deletion of historical records.
- Draft/unlinked files: propose a 30-day reconciliation grace period after verifying no current or historical revision references the object; approved operator deletion only, with dry-run report and audit record.
- Superseded evidence: remain restricted while required for audit or appeals; do not delete just because a new revision exists. Retention/deletion process must cover database, Storage, linked provider files and backups according to institutional rules.
- Existing implementation deliberately preserves revisions and has no retention automation or approved deletion process. Do not enable real student uploads until a lawful operational deletion/hold procedure, retention schedule and responsible owner are confirmed.

Validate individual lab and group + individual examples, desktop/mobile, independent review, scope and stale source behavior, real local scanner/storage, audit traceability, no production data. Real pilot begins only after technical and policy sign-off; record actual date, responsible staff and findings.

- RUPP authorized records/data owner: pending confirmation of exact retention, notice, deletion and access policy.
- Technical owner: pending latest-head CI, isolated staging tests, real Storage/scanner verification, performance and security review.
