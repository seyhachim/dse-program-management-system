# DSE PMS Academic and Engineering Constitution

Status: proposed repository development baseline, pending programme maintainer review. Revision: 0.1 (2026-11-26). Scope: all PMS contributors and agents; not an institutional regulation or accreditation claim.

## Authority and use

Applicable law and verified approved RUPP policies govern institutional decisions; approved DSE curriculum, programme policies and course specifications govern within delegated authority. CDIO and AUN-QA are external educational/QA frameworks, not substitutes for institutional policy. Where sources conflict, are absent, or are not accessible, record the gap and request an authorized academic decision; do not silently choose a grading or progression rule. Use [REFERENCES](REFERENCES.md) and [DECISION-REGISTER](DECISION-REGISTER.md). A merge approves repository guidance only, not new RUPP policy.

## Development invariants

- AC-001: Design academic functions around explicit, measurable, versioned outcomes.
- AC-002: Align teaching, learning activities, assessment and outcomes; never infer alignment from names alone.
- AC-003: Separate final course grade, outcome attainment and programme QA metrics.
- AC-004: Require explicit, valid and traceable evidence; distinguish direct assessment from supporting evidence.
- AC-005: Attendance/participation is not automatically outcome evidence.
- AC-006: Preserve exact approved versions, historical records and audit history; corrections are authorized and traceable.
- AC-007: Distinguish missing, ungraded, absent, exempt and assessed zero according to approved policy; do not impute attainment.
- AC-008: Distinguish individual and group evidence; do not infer individual mastery from membership.
- AC-009: Map engineering competencies explicitly and distinguish exposure, practice and assessment.
- AC-010: Make calculations reproducible with policy version, inputs, denominator, scope and provenance.
- AC-011: AI assistance is not academic approval; require accountable human review for consequential academic decisions.
- AC-012: Enable continuous improvement with evidence, action, ownership and follow-up.
- AC-013: Version institutional policy and applicability; never silently apply changes retrospectively.
- AC-014: Protect student data and examinations; authorization is enforced server-side and at the database as applicable.
- AC-015: Prefer practical workflows that reduce duplicated data entry without undermining validity.

## Before academic changes

Read [OBE](OBE.md), [CDIO](CDIO.md), [AUN-QA](AUN-QA.md), [RUPP policies](RUPP-POLICIES.md), and [assessment policy](ASSESSMENT-POLICY.md) as applicable. Identify the issue, affected cohort/offering/specification version, relevant AC IDs, authority and locator, evidence flow, permissions, historical compatibility and tests. Distinguish external guidance, approved local policy, existing product behavior and proposed choices. State non-applicable frameworks with reasons; do not invent clause references. When unresolved policy would change academic meaning, stop that slice pending review while permitting independent technical fixes. Follow root AGENTS.md engineering gates.

## Ownership

Proposed steward: DSE programme coordinator with designated academic/QA reviewer and repository maintainer. Policy decisions need the authorized institutional/programme owner, not an AI or a code merge. Update this guidance by reviewed PR with decision record and affected tests. Phase 1 is documentation only; automated enforcement and audits are later work.
