# AUN-QA programme-level alignment

Status: external guide version 4.0 verified at framework level; proposed PMS evidence mapping, not accreditation determination. Source AUN-01 in [references](REFERENCES.md).

| Programme guide criterion | Candidate PMS evidence |
|---|---|
| 1 Expected learning outcomes | approved PLO/CLO definitions, alignment, outcome evidence |
| 2 Programme structure and content | curriculum versions, course specs, mapping and reviews |
| 3 Teaching and learning approach | weekly plans, teaching methods, activities and review |
| 4 Student assessment | assessment policy, instruments, moderation, results and feedback |
| 5 Academic staff | workload, qualifications, development and verified annual records |
| 6 Student support services | advising, opportunities and support records with consent/access controls |
| 7 Facilities and infrastructure | verified resource evidence; gaps may require external records |
| 8 Output and outcomes | approved aggregate metrics, stakeholder feedback and improvement actions |

The table is a product interpretation, not a clause-by-clause audit. For a specific requirement, inspect the official criterion/subcriterion and record its locator before claiming coverage. Do not derive QA scores or declare compliance from data presence.

QA evidence needs provenance, authorized reviewer, exact scope/time/version, status, reproducible calculations and traceable underlying artifacts. Distinguish evidence availability from adequacy; report missing or unverified items transparently. Restrict personal data and sensitive documents; redact/aggregate for authorized reporting. Append corrections or snapshots; do not rewrite history. Link review findings to responsible owner, action, deadline and follow-up evaluation.
