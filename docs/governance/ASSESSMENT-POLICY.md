# Assessment and evidence development policy

Status: repository guidance; institutional grading and rubric particulars pending authorized verification. Read [constitution](CONSTITUTION.md), [OBE](OBE.md), [RUPP policies](RUPP-POLICIES.md) and existing [grade/CLO separation](../assessment-grade-clo-separation.md), [instrument library](../assessment-instrument-library.md).

## Boundaries
- Course grading uses approved assessment weights; CLO evidence uses explicit valid mappings and a separately approved aggregation policy. Do not introduce an AUN grade weight or count supporting-only mappings as direct evidence.
- Rubrics evaluate defined criteria/performance levels; examination marking schemes use questions/subquestions and mark allocations. Reuse the assessment instrument library, not a duplicate model.
- Proposed default for new OBE rubric drafts: institutionally verified four-level scale, positive weighted criterion maxima, explicit criterion-level CLO mapping. The exact labels, descriptors, mark conversion and applicability require authorized approval (#1361); do not hard-code or retrospectively migrate them.
- Preserve null/ungraded vs scored zero; reject incomplete, duplicate, out-of-range and stale-version scores. A calculated total must be derived consistently from the applicable marks and validated before publication.
- Exam variants must resolve to the exact assigned immutable paper/instrument version; use question-specific maximum and mappings. Ensure equivalent intended outcomes and comparability through academic moderation, not assumed equal difficulty. Keep actual questions/answers/keys private. Coordinate #1355/#1371; do not assume draft features are live.
- Group deliverables may contribute to course grade as approved; individual attainment requires attributable evidence. Attendance is grade-only by default unless valid explicit outcome evidence is approved.
- Preserve publication, finalization, correction and review history. Corrections must be authorized, traceable and consistent with the original versions; regenerate new QA snapshots without erasing previous ones.

## Verification cases
Local grade vs CLO calculations, zero vs missing, criterion/question score boundaries, direct vs supporting evidence, exact-version variant assignment, individual/group attribution, unauthorized access and public leakage, historical snapshots, moderation and publication locks. Use synthetic data for testing. Approved policies determine numeric scales, pass rules and rounding.
